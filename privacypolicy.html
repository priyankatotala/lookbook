Privacy Policy

Lookbook "the App" provides Shopify users the ability to create simple Lookbooks to display on their store and assign product markers to the pages "the Service" to merchants who use Shopify to power their stores. Personal Information the App Collects
When you install the App, we are automatically able to access certain types of information from your Shopify account: products, themes, pages API to mark the lookbooks with the products.

We collect personal information using the following technologies: “Cookies” are data files that are placed on your device or computer and often include an anonymous unique identifier. For more information about cookies, and how to disable cookies, visit http://www.allaboutcookies.org. “Log files” track actions occurring on the Site.
